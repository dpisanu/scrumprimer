Whether you're completely new to Scrum or looking to deepen your understanding, this is a place to explore the ideas, principles, and practices behind the Scrum framework.

Here you'll find two complementary resources:

## A Lightweight Guide to the Theory and Practice of Scrum

A practical introduction to Scrum that goes beyond the basics. Discover how Scrum works, why it emphasizes self-managing teams and short feedback cycles, and how its roles, events, and artifacts come together to support effective product development.

With real-world examples, illustrations, and practical guidance, the Scrum Primer helps bridge the gap between understanding Scrum and putting it into practice.

## A Scrum Overview Picture

Sometimes a picture says more than a thousand words. The Scrum Overview provides a visual guide to the Scrum framework, showing how its roles, events, and artifacts fit together in an iterative cycle of delivering value, gathering feedback, and continuously improving.

## Created by practitioners, shared by a community

Originally authored by **Pete Deemer, Gabrielle Benefield, Craig Larman, and Bas Vodde**, the Scrum Primer has grown through community contributions and translations, making Scrum knowledge accessible to people around the world.

Rooted in an **open-source and collaborative mindset**, this corner celebrates sharing knowledge, crediting its creators, and learning together.
